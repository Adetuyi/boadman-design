A host puts up a prize before anyone enters. Entry fees are then added to it as players join, so two numbers sit on every competition and they mean different things.

The stake

Both players' stakes are held by Boadman from the start of the match until it settles. Neither side can withdraw or spend them while the match is running.

If you lose, the coins you staked are gone. Only stake what you can afford to lose.

Terms: stakes

The result

Players compete in the game. The result of the match decides the prize.

An entrant can dispute a result. When that happens, settlement waits for the dispute to close rather than running on schedule.

Terms: results and disputes

Settlement

Commission is deducted here, from every settled competition. On a player-hosted tournament the host's fee comes out at the same point, and the remainder goes to the winners.

The rate that applies to you is shown before you confirm any entry. The current rates are set out in what it costs.

The hold

Art slot: hold-plate

A prize is held for 48 hours before it can be spent, so other entrants can raise a complaint. The prize hold and the window for raising a dispute are different mechanics, and they are not the same clock.

While a prize is held, a complaint or a review can freeze it, and it can be reversed. It is not yours to spend until the hold has run.

Terms: prize holds

Cash out

Money leaves only to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account. Identity checks are required before you can cash out, and again at higher amounts.

A payout account whose name does not match stops the payout. So does an identity check you have not completed.

Terms: payouts

Where it can stop.

Four things move money off the rail. Each is a real mechanic in the product, and none of them carries a promised turnaround, because Boadman does not publish one.

  • A complaint raised while a prize is held

    Another entrant contests the outcome before the hold has run. The prize stays where it is until that complaint is closed, and closing it can move the money back.

    A dispute found to be frivolous carries a fee, charged to whoever raised it.

  • A transaction pulled for review

    A deposit, a settlement or a payout can be held for anti-money-laundering review. While that review is open the money does not move, and you will not be told the detail of why.

  • A bank account in a different name

    Payouts go only to an account in the same legal name as the Boadman account. A mismatch is not a delay. The payout stops until you supply an account that matches.

  • An identity check not finished

    Identity checks are required before you can cash out, and again at higher amounts. Until the check that applies is completed, the cash-out does not run.

What it costs.

Commission is the platform's cut of a settled competition. Every rate below is stated with the sentence that qualifies it, because a rate can change and the one that binds you is the one shown at the point you confirm.

Commission is currently 5% on a 1v1 challenge; the rate that applies to you is shown before you confirm any entry.

Commission is currently 8% on a player-hosted tournament, taken alongside the host's fee; the rate that applies to you is shown before you confirm any entry.

Commission is currently 6% on a brand-hosted tournament; the rate that applies is shown to the host before the competition is published.

A dispute found to be frivolous currently carries a 10% fee; the fee that applies is shown before you raise one.

Commission is deducted from every settled competition, and a dispute found to be frivolous carries a fee. The rate and the fee that apply to you are shown before you confirm an entry or raise a dispute, and the current figures are set out in the Terms.
