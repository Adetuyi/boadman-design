Player

“It won’t pay out.”

A reasonable prior. We cannot prove a future payout, so instead here is what the money actually does, what we take, and the three things that stop it.

The rake, in public.

Every settled competition has commission taken off it. Here is where, and how much.

1v1 challenge

Commission on a 1v1 challenge is currently 10%, and the exact rate that applies to you is shown before you confirm the stake. Commission is taken from a settled 1v1 challenge, and the exact rate that applies to you is shown before you confirm the stake.

Player-hosted tournament

A player-hosted tournament settles as the host fee, then commission of currently 12%, then the remainder to winners, and the rate is shown before you enter. A player-hosted tournament settles as the host fee, then commission, then the remainder to winners, and the rate is shown before you enter.

Brand-hosted tournament

Commission on a brand-hosted tournament is currently 8%, and the rate is shown to the brand before the competition is published. Commission is taken from a settled brand-hosted tournament, and the rate is shown to the brand before the competition is published.

Frivolous dispute

A dispute judged frivolous currently carries a fee of 5%, and the fee that would apply is shown before you raise one. A dispute judged frivolous carries a fee, and the fee that would apply is shown before you raise one.

Rates change. The one on your screen at the moment you confirm is the one that applies, and it is the one written into the Terms.

Three things that stop your money.

A complaint

Another entrant raises one while the prize is still held. The prize stays where it is until the complaint is resolved, and it can be reversed. The window for raising one is set in the Terms.

A review

Activity on an account can be put under anti-money-laundering review before money leaves. While that review is open, the payout does not go out, and nobody can tell you when it will.

A name that does not match

Money leaves only to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account. If the names do not match, the payout does not leave, and no amount of asking changes that.

All three are written down. Terms and FAQs.

Publisher

“You’ll put my game next to something embarrassing.”

Today we hold rights to no game at all. There is exactly one route by which a title arrives on this platform, and it starts with you.

publisher-panel . 4:5 . 1200x1500
  • Register

    A studio registers and verifies. Nothing happens before that step completes.

  • Grant

    Rights are granted for named titles, in exchange for a royalty share of the platform commission taken on competitions played on them.

  • Publish

    An administrator sets the cover image for the title. Until that happens, competitions render without art, which is what every card on this page is doing right now.

The parts that are not attractive. There is no publisher signup route. The only door is a waitlist, and a person reads it. And your royalty is a share of platform commission, not of the prize pool, so if commission on a competition is small, so is the royalty. Terms.

More objections.

What if I win and the money does not arrive?
A prize is held for 48 hours before it can be spent, so other entrants can raise a complaint. If one is raised, the prize stays where it is until it is resolved, and it can be reversed. If nothing is raised and no review is open, it becomes spendable and you can request a payout. The window for raising a complaint is set in the Terms.
Who decides a dispute?
Boadman does. A complaint is reviewed by Boadman staff and the outcome can go against you. If it is judged frivolous, a fee is charged to the account that raised it, and the fee that would apply is shown before you raise one. Terms.
Why do you need my identity?
Identity checks are required before you can cash out, and again at higher amounts. Payouts go only to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account, so the name on the account has to be checkable. Privacy.
What is a coin actually worth?
One coin is 10p today. Coins are bought with real money, that rate can change, you can lose the coins you stake, and commission is deducted from every settled competition. Terms.
