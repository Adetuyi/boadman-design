01

Two players.One pot.

Five steps, and none of them are hidden from you.

The same five steps run a 64 player tournament and a single 1v1 challenge. The only thing that changes is how many stakes go into the pot.

  1. 01

    Buy coins

    Coins are bought by card at the current rate, 1 coin = 10p, and land in your wallet. They are bought with real money.

  2. 02

    Enter, and the stake leaves

    Entering a competition moves the stake out of your wallet immediately. A tournament with an entry fee of exactly 0 coins takes nothing.

  3. 03

    Play the match

    The match is played in the game itself. Boadman holds both stakes from the start of play until settlement. Neither side can spend, withdraw or re-stake them while the match runs.

  4. 04

    Settle

    The pot pays the winner, minus platform commission. The losing stake pays the winner and is not returned. The commission rate in force is shown before you enter.

  5. 05

    Hold, then cash out

    A prize is held for 48 hours before it can be spent, then matures on its own. Cash out goes only to a bank account in the same legal name as your Boadman account.

Identity checks are required before a first cash out, and again at higher amounts.

03

48 hoursbefore it is yours.

A prize exists before it can be spent. Both halves of that sentence are printed at the same size below.

What the hold does

  • At settlement the pot pays the winner, minus platform commission.
  • The prize is then held for 48 hours before it can be spent, so other entrants have a window in which to raise a complaint about cheating.
  • It matures automatically when the window closes. Nobody has to release it by hand.
  • From the start of a match until it settles, neither side can spend, withdraw or re-stake the pot.
  • The same 48 hour hold runs on a player hosted tournament prize and on a 1v1 challenge prize.

What can stop the money

  • Losing. A lost stake pays the winner and is not returned.
  • A complaint raised inside the 48 hour window freezes the prize until an admin rules on it.
  • A dispute ruled frivolous carries a fee, deducted from the person who filed it.
  • A money laundering review can freeze a cash out while it runs.
  • A bank account whose name does not match the account holder does not get paid. Identity checks are required before a first cash out, and again at higher amounts.

Boadman does not run the game. It holds the pot, records the result and rules on complaints.
