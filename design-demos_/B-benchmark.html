The route a coin takes

Seven steps from card to bank account.

Nothing here is hidden behind a sign-up. This is the whole path, including the parts that cost you.

  1. 01

    Buy coins

    Coins are bought with a card, with real money. Current rate: 1 coin = 10p. Coins are the unit for every stake and every prize on the platform.

  2. 02

    Enter a competition

    Entering moves the stake out of your wallet immediately. From that moment it is committed, whatever happens next.

  3. 03

    The pot is held

    From the start of a match until it settles, both sides' stakes are held by Boadman. Neither player can spend, withdraw or re-stake them during play.

  4. 04

    Play the match

    The match is played in the game. Boadman does not run it, referee it live, or decide it. It holds the pot and records the result.

  5. 05

    Settlement

    The pot pays the winner, minus platform commission. The rate that applies to a competition is shown before you enter and is set out in the Terms. The loser's stake is gone.

  6. 06

    A 48 hour hold

    A prize is held for 48 hours so other entrants can raise a complaint about cheating, then matures automatically. A complaint raised inside that window freezes the prize until an admin rules on it.

  7. 07

    Cash out

    Payment goes only to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account. Identity checks are required before a first cash out, and again at higher amounts.

Before you stake

Six things that stop the money.

Plenty of platforms bury this. It is easier to read it now than to find it out later, so here it is at the same size as everything else on the page.

01

You lose

A lost stake pays the winner. It is not returned, and there is no partial refund of an entry fee once a competition has started.

02

A complaint is raised

A complaint made inside the 48 hour hold freezes the prize until an admin rules on it. A prize can be reversed by that ruling.

03

A dispute is frivolous

A dispute ruled frivolous carries a fee, and that fee is deducted from the person who filed it.

04

A review is running

A money laundering review can freeze a cash out for as long as it takes to complete.

05

The names do not match

A bank account whose name does not match the account holder's does not get paid. Payment goes to your own legal name or nowhere.

06

Identity is not confirmed

Identity checks are required before a first cash out, and again at higher amounts. Until they pass, the money stays where it is.

Commission is deducted on every settlement, so the pot a winner receives is smaller than the pot shown on the card. All of this is set out in full in the Terms.

Brands put coins into prize pools and host competitions in front of a competitive audience that turned up to play, not to be advertised at. The card shows what you put up, so your contribution is visible on the board rather than described in a caption.

For publishers and studios

Nothing runs
without you.

Publishers license their titles to the platform and earn a royalty share of platform commission. This is the only mechanism by which Boadman gains the right to run competitions on a game: no licence, no competition, no exceptions.

No publisher has onboarded yet, so the board you see above is a layout, not an inventory. The door today is a waitlist, and it is a short one.

Keep it a choice you are making.
