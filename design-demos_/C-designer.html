01 Competition platform for players 18+ only

The match decides.Nothing else.

Boadman runs 1v1 challenges and tournaments for players aged 18 and over. You buy coins, you stake them, and the result of the match decides who takes the pot. Boadman holds the pot while the match runs and records the result. It does not play, and it does not pick the winner.

03 1v1 challenge

Or callsomeone out.

One match. Two stakes. One pot. From the moment the match starts until it settles, both stakes are held by Boadman. Neither side can spend them, withdraw them or re-stake them while it runs.

The losing stake pays the winner and is not returned. Commission is deducted from the pot at settlement, and the prize is then held for 48 hours before it can be spent.

04 Where the money goes

Every step,and what can stop it.

Six steps between a card payment and a bank transfer. Each one is written here next to the thing that can interrupt it, at the same size, because that is the half most platforms leave off the page.

  1. 01

    You buy coins

    Coins are bought with a card and land in your wallet. 1 coin = 10p.
    The other halfCoins are bought with real money. They are the unit you stake, not a reward, and nothing on Boadman is earned by signing up.
  2. 02

    You enter

    Entering a competition moves the stake out of your wallet immediately.
    The other halfA lost stake pays the winner and is not returned to you. There is no partial refund once a match has started.
  3. 03

    You play

    From the start of the match until it settles, both sides' stakes are held by Boadman. Neither side can spend, withdraw or re-stake them during play.
    The other halfThe match is played in the game itself. Boadman does not run the game. It holds the pot and records the result.
  4. 04

    It settles

    At settlement the pot pays the winner.
    The other halfPlatform commission is deducted from every settlement. The rate is set by Boadman and shown on the competition page before you enter.
  5. 05

    The prize holds for 48 hours

    A prize is held for 48 hours before it can be spent, so other entrants can raise a complaint about cheating. It matures automatically when the window closes.
    The other halfA complaint raised inside that window freezes the prize until an admin rules on it. A dispute ruled frivolous carries a fee, deducted from the person who filed it.
  6. 06

    You cash out

    Winnings convert back to money and go to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account.
    The other halfA bank account whose name does not match the account holder does not get paid. Identity checks are required before a first cash-out and again at higher amounts, and a money-laundering review can freeze a cash-out while it runs.

Holding a pot during a match is a mechanic, not a guarantee. Boadman describes what it does with money and publishes nothing about its own solvency, supervision or standing.

Players stake. Brands fund pools. Publishers license the titles the competitions are built on. One of these three is you.

  • 03A prize holds for 48 hours so complaints can be raised, then matures on its own.
  • 04Cash out to a bank account in your own legal name.

No publisher,no game.

Boadman cannot run a competition on a title it holds no rights to. A publisher licence is the only mechanism that changes that, which is why this page names no game and shows no game artwork.

  • 01You license the title. Boadman builds and runs the competition around it.

  • 03No publisher has onboarded yet, and no publisher signup route exists. The waitlist is the only door.
  • 04Terms, rates and royalty share are agreed in writing before anything is listed.

Royalty is a share of commission actually collected on settled competitions, so it depends entirely on whether players enter and finish them. Terms

06 If it stops being a game

Know whereto stop.

Staking coins puts real money at risk every time. If that stops feeling like a game, the number below is answered by people who are not us.

GamCare is an independent service. It is not run by Boadman, it is not paid by Boadman, and it will talk to you confidentially about gambling harm without asking you to keep an account open.

Be clear about what Boadman itself offers today: the only player control on the platform is a manual account suspension you request by email. There are no deposit limits, no loss limits, no session reminders, no reality checks and no cooling-off timers. If you need your account stopped, write to us and a person will action it.

Everything on Boadman is for adults aged 18 and over. Accounts are checked, and cash-out requires identity verification in your own legal name.
