Six steps from a card to a bank account, and the brake on each one.

Plenty of people reading this have paid into a platform that never paid out. So here is the whole route, with the parts that stop the money printed at the same size as the parts that move it.

  1. Buy coins

    A card payment puts coins in your wallet. They sit there until you enter something.

    BrakeCard payments are not switched on yet. Boadman is reachable and accounts can be created, but deposits and payouts stay gated until our payment processor approves us.

  2. Enter

    Entering a 1v1 challenge or a tournament moves the stake out of your wallet at once, not at the end.

    BrakeThat stake is gone from your balance from the moment you enter. If you lose, it pays the other player and does not come back.

  3. Play

    From the start of the match until it settles, Boadman holds both stakes. The match itself happens in the game.

    BrakeNeither side can spend, withdraw or re-stake that money during play, and that includes you. Boadman does not run the game, so it cannot referee it live: it holds the pot and records the result.

  4. Settle

    The result decides the pot. It pays the winner in coins.

    BrakeCommission is deducted from the pot at settlement on every competition, win or lose. It is not a fee we waive. Terms

Hold, then cash out

A prize is held for 48 hours before it can be spent, so other entrants have a window to raise a complaint about cheating. Nobody has to release it: it matures on its own when the window closes. Cash-out then goes only to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account.

What stops it. A complaint raised inside the window freezes the prize until an admin rules on it. A dispute ruled frivolous carries a fee, taken from whoever filed it. A money-laundering review can freeze a cash-out while it runs, an identity check is required before a first cash-out and again at higher amounts, and a bank account in a different name does not get paid at all.

18+, and a short list of what we do not have.

Boadman has no deposit limits, no loss limits, no session reminders, no reality checks, no cooling-off timers and no self-exclusion. None of that is built yet. The only control that exists today is a manual account suspension, which you request by email and a person actions.

Staking is a real cost. Money you put into a competition can leave and not come back, and no part of this page should be read as an expectation of getting it back with more.
