Six moves. The balance is spendable in two of them.

Money in a competition is nearly always in someone else's hands, including ours. Here is every stage, and what stops it at each one.

  • Spendable
  • Committed
  • Held

Buy

Spendable

You buy coins with a card and they land in your wallet. 1 coin costs 10p.

Card payments are switched off while processor approval is outstanding, so no coins are being sold today.

Enter

Committed

Entering a competition moves the stake out of your wallet straight away.

From that moment you cannot spend it, withdraw it or stake it anywhere else.

Play

Held

The match happens in the game itself. Boadman does not run the game. It holds both stakes and records the result.

Neither player can reach the pot while the match is running.

Settle

Committed

The pot pays the winner, and commission is taken out of it first.

The losing stake is gone. It pays the winner and is not returned.

Mature

Held

A prize is held for 48 hours after settlement, then matures on its own.

Another entrant can raise a complaint inside that window and the prize freezes until an admin rules.

Cash out

Spendable

Money goes to a bank account in the same legal name as the Boadman account.

A name that does not match is not paid. Identity checks and money laundering reviews can hold it.

Every stage above is set out in the Terms.

Held for 48 hours

A prize is held for 48 hours after a competition settles. Nobody has to approve it. When the window closes it matures on its own and becomes spendable.

Frozen until an admin rules

Another entrant can raise a complaint about cheating inside those 48 hours. The prize freezes until an admin rules on it, and a complaint judged frivolous carries a fee taken from whoever filed it.

Holds, complaints and dispute fees are set out in the Terms.

Commission comes out of every settled pot.

A prize is always smaller than the pool it came from. The deduction happens at settlement, before the winner is paid, on every competition that settles.

The rate is set by Boadman and can change, so no rate is printed here. The figure that applies to a competition is the one held on the server at the moment it settles.

Put up the prize pool. Your name runs the competition.

No game is here until a publisher puts it here.

Publishers license their titles to Boadman and take a royalty share of platform commission. That licence is the only mechanism by which any game can be competed on here, which is why the board above is empty.

A royalty is a share of commission, not a share of the stakes, and commission only exists once a competition settles. There is no publisher signup route yet. The waitlist is the only door open.

Licensing and royalty terms are in the Terms.
